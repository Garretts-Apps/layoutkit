Step 5

Switcher, from the container

Children sit in a row above 30rem and take the full row below it. at picks another threshold: xs 20rem, sm 24rem, lg 40rem, xl 48rem, 2xl 64rem. Five or more children each take a row unless you set limit.

Step 7

Snap scrolling

A horizontal lk-scroll-area snaps unless snap="off". slide="peek" shows the next item. Give the region a tabindex and a name so it is a keyboard stop.
